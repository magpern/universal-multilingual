## Fixed — multi-wake job continuation

`BackgroundTranslationWorker` processes a job's segments in bounded wakes of
`MAX_ITEMS_PER_WAKE` (10) so a single Action Scheduler run stays within request
limits. When a wake hit that cap with segments still queued, the worker returned
**without scheduling the next wake**. The hourly maintenance sweep only recovers
crashed/stale-lease jobs — it does not re-dispatch a cleanly lease-released job —
so the job sat at `running` and made no further progress.

Effect: a page translated with **Translate with AI** (or a bulk job from
**Translate selected with AI**) that had more than 10 translatable segments
translated only its first 10 and then appeared stuck, with the editor's Quality
checks panel flagging the remaining segments as `empty_translation`.

The worker now re-enqueues its own next wake while claimable items remain,
guarded by a "made progress this wake" check so a zero-progress wake cannot
hot-loop. Retry-waiting items keep their existing delayed re-enqueue. The
production worker is already constructed with the scheduler, so no wiring
change was needed.

Regression test: `JobsWorkerTest::test_worker_reschedules_next_wake_when_claimable_items_remain`
(15-segment page → wake 1 completes 10 and arms the next wake → wake 2
finalises).
